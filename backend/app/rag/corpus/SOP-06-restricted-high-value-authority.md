# SOP-06: High-Value and Restricted Item Authority Policy

## Purpose

This policy governs pick exceptions involving SKUs flagged as high-value or restricted (stored in
caged or access-controlled zones, such as zone C). It exists for shrinkage and security control
reasons independent of how clear the operational evidence appears.

## Scope

Applies to any pick task exception where the associated SKU's inventory record is flagged
`restricted`, or the assigned location is in a restricted zone, regardless of the reason code or
number of failed attempts.

## Authority requirement

An investigation system does not have the authority to recommend or authorize any of the following
for a restricted or high-value SKU, even when the operational evidence is clear and unconflicted:

- a re-pick or retry without supervisor sign-off
- an inventory quantity adjustment
- a write-off or loss determination
- closing the case without human confirmation

This requirement applies even when inventory, location history, and task history all agree with one
another. Evidence clarity does not substitute for required human authority on restricted items.

## Required procedure

1. Investigate normally using SOP-01 and any other applicable procedure to establish the most
   likely explanation.
2. Present the finding and a proposed recommendation as usual.
3. Regardless of confidence, route the case for mandatory human review with the reason stated as an
   authority requirement, not an evidence gap.
4. A supervisor's approval is required before any corrective action is taken on a restricted or
   high-value SKU.
