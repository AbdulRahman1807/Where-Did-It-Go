# SOP-07: Short-Pick and Partial Fulfillment Ambiguity Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure applies when a pick task returns fewer units than requested (a short pick) rather
than a complete failure to find the item.

## Legitimate causes of a short pick

A short pick is not automatically a discrepancy. Common legitimate causes include:

- a concurrent reservation of some units for another order placed shortly before the pick attempt
- units set aside for quality inspection or damage review
- a cycle count adjustment that reduced the recorded quantity shortly before the attempt

If a concurrent reservation or adjustment event explains the reduced quantity, and the timing is
consistent with the pick attempt, the short pick is likely legitimate rather than a data error.

## Standard orders

For standard-priority orders, when a legitimate concurrent cause is identified and the picked
quantity is confirmed accurate, the recommended action is to fulfill the order with the available
quantity and flag the remainder as backordered. Human review is not required solely because a
legitimate cause was identified.

## Expedited orders

For expedited-priority orders, a short pick should not be silently partially fulfilled even when a
legitimate concurrent cause is identified. Because expedited orders carry a higher service
commitment, human confirmation is required before deciding whether to partially ship, hold for full
quantity, or reprioritize the reservation that caused the shortage. This is a procedural requirement
based on order priority, not a sign that the evidence is unclear.

## No explanatory event found

If no concurrent reservation, inspection hold, or adjustment event explains the shortfall, treat it
as an unresolved inventory discrepancy under SOP-02 rather than assuming a legitimate cause.
